📖 How to play

  • Pass the phone 📱. Everyone gets questions at their own level.
  • Build each player's face in the Character Creator: colour, eyes, nose, mouth, teeth, glasses and hats. Then add a name and school year.
  • Kids can mix year groups. Tap more years on their card (e.g. Year 4 + Year 5).
  • Wrong answer or time runs out = lose a 💙
  • ⭐ Golden Ball: answer right to win a life back!
  • ⚡ Every 30 questions = Level Up: questions get harder and the timer drops by 2 seconds (never below 5).
  • ⭐ Points: right answers score more when you're fast, on a streak or on a higher level.
  • 📊 Every level has its own scoreboard. See the Hall of Fame.
  • Last player standing is the Pink Ponk Champion 🏆
